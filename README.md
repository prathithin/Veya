## What it does
Veya brings dance practice, exercise resources, and AI-powered feedback together in one mobile app. Dancers upload a video of themselves, and Veya analyzes their movement with the Gemini API and gives coaching feedback.

## Features
- **Home dashboard:** card-based layout (inspired by Apple Health) for quick access to stretches, video uploads, the gallery, and chat
- **Stretch library:** exercises organized by dance style, like hip hop and ballet
- **AI feedback:** review your uploaded video with timeline markers, an accuracy indicator, and written coaching notes
- **Coaching chat:** ask the AI coaching assistant questions about your practice
